# Critique: Apple

## What it does well
Apple's product pages are the reference point for "confident restraint" — huge type, one idea per scroll-beat, generous whitespace, and scroll-triggered reveals that never feel gimmicky because they're always in service of showing the product, never decoration for its own sake. Section-to-section rhythm is metronomic: viewers always know roughly how a new section will introduce itself.

## What to borrow
- **One idea per viewport** — resist the urge to cram Skills + Projects context into the same scroll-beat. Each section should have a single, unmistakable focal point (`design/LayoutSystem.md`).
- **Type-first hierarchy** — Apple rarely needs decorative UI chrome because the typography itself carries the hierarchy. Directly reinforces why Mecha Typography's display face should stay reserved for a few high-impact moments (`design/Typography.md`), not spread thin.
- **Scroll discipline** — motion always reveals content the user is about to read, never plays *before* there's something to show.

## Where to diverge (this is the important part)
Apple is defined by the *absence* of a distinct graphic system — no persistent iconography, no recurring motif, because the product itself is the visual identity. ManhnhaPortfolio doesn't have a physical product to lean on, which is exactly why the Mecha Typography system (corner brackets, section index, status tags — `design/MechaTypographySystem.md`) exists: it's the recurring identity mark Apple doesn't need but this site does. Copying Apple's "let whitespace do the talking" literally would leave the site with no distinguishing mark at all — the opposite of the goal.
