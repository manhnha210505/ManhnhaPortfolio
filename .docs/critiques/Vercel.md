# Critique: Vercel

## What it does well
Vercel's site is a strong reference for **restraint under a black/white-dominant palette** — most of the visual interest comes from typography, grid structure, and very sparing use of accent color, not from heavy visual effects. Their product screenshots are treated as first-class content, framed cleanly rather than floating in a busy scene.

## What to borrow
- **Grid-as-visible-structure** — Vercel occasionally lets the underlying grid lines show as a deliberate texture rather than hiding them. This is a natural, low-risk way to introduce a "schematic/blueprint" feel (relevant to `design/MechaTypographySystem.md`) without needing a full illustrated HUD — worth prototyping as a subtle background treatment.
- **Sparing accent use** — accent color appears at decision points (CTAs, active states) and almost nowhere else. Good calibration check for `ColorSystem.md`'s cyan accent: if it's showing up as a large fill in more than 1-2 places per section, it's probably overused.

## Where to diverge
Vercel's system is intentionally *quiet* — it's built for a platform brand where the product itself needs to feel invisible/frictionless. ManhnhaPortfolio needs to actively demonstrate a person's technical identity, so it can afford (and per the chosen direction, should) be louder in specific, controlled ways — the Mecha Typography system elements (section index, status tags) are exactly the kind of "loud but disciplined" details Vercel's brand deliberately avoids.
