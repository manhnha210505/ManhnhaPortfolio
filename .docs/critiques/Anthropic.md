# Critique: Anthropic

## What it does well
Anthropic's site favors a warm, slightly literary tone and a restrained, almost print-inspired typographic system — serif/humanist pairings, calm color, minimal ornamentation. It communicates "serious research organization" through *tone and typographic quality* rather than through visual system complexity.

## What to borrow
- **Typographic quality as the primary credibility signal** — proof that a highly technical organization can communicate seriousness without any HUD/technical-system dressing at all, purely through type choice and pacing. Useful as a sanity check: even with Mecha Typography's system elements, the underlying typographic craft (`design/Typography.md`'s body/UI typeface) needs to hold up on its own, because that's still doing most of the credibility work, not the corner brackets.
- **Calm, unhurried pacing** in how claims are made — no hype language, specific and measured. Directly matches `branding/VoiceAndTone.md`.

## Where to diverge
Anthropic's system is essentially the *anti*-Mecha-Typography approach — warm, literary, low-tech-signaling despite being a highly technical company. This is worth noting as a reminder that "serious and technical" doesn't require a HUD system at all; ManhnhaPortfolio is choosing Mecha Typography deliberately for differentiation and identity reasons (`design/DesignPhilosophy.md`), not because it's the only way to read as credible. Keep that context in mind if the system ever feels forced in a particular section — the fallback of "just let good typography and honest content carry it" (Anthropic's approach) is always available and legitimate.
