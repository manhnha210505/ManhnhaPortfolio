# Storytelling

## Project case-study structure
Every project in the Projects section follows the same narrative shape, reinforcing the "DS engineer, not just developer" identity:
1. **Problem** — what question or business/technical need existed.
2. **Approach** — what was actually built/modeled/engineered, and key decisions made (this is where technical depth shows).
3. **Impact** — measurable or qualitative outcome; be honest about scale (a side project's "impact" can be learning/technical achievement, not fabricated business metrics).

## Site-level narrative arc
Hero (who) → About (why/how they think) → Skills (what they're capable of) → Projects (proof) → Contact (call to action). Each section should feel like the natural next question a reader would ask, not an arbitrary list.
